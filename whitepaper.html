1. Protocol Summary & Architecture

XTAP Network is an enterprise-grade cloud-mining protocol built on the BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20). By replacing traditional hardware-intensive Proof-of-Work (PoW) with distributed Proof-of-Active-Mining (PoAM), XTAP enables mobile devices to operate synchronized cloud rigs without device battery drainage, physical overheating, or carbon overhead.

2. Cloud Rig Mechanics & Hash Dynamics

  • Automated Production Blocks: Rig cycles execute autonomously over 8-to-24 hour computational windows.
  • Temporary Vault Claiming: Mined units accrue inside high-speed storage buffers; miners claim batches before capacity thresholds to ensure unhalted extraction.
  • Hash Upgrades: Mined points are reinvested directly to scale processing frequency (GH/s) and rig capacity.
